Why do I

Need help with everything

I dance and I sing

I don’t know how to do

A damn thing

I thought I changed

Really I haven’t

I inflate myself

A balloon ready to pop

And when it does

Nobody minds

I could die tomorrow

And the earth would be lighter

Weight lifted off its own shoulders

And my head off my own

I don’t talk

I am scared what other people think

About me

I don’t do enough

My everything is existent

Within a false sense of identity

All I do is act smart

All I do is pretend I know so much

Then when someone asks me to prove it

I rely on other things

I’m dirty on the inside

The grime

The grime eats away on my consciousness

I don’t know how to think

When I pose myself with a question

A question of what?

I don’t know

It could be anything

A song, a creation, a work of art

Nothing escapes through the tips of my fingers nor my tongue nor my eyes nor my teeth

I sit there and give up before I even began

Then when I do try

When I think I have something

I stop and I don’t understand why

My jaw hang loose

The buckshot of my Remington chambered twelve

Swathes through my skull turning a gray matter and like wet itch

It doesn’t stop until it’s done

It’s all so unfair

Why couldn’t it have been

Another time?

Another place?

A different start?

I could learn from the ground up

But I’m afraid of losing comfort

I’m afraid to do anything that actually requires

A steady back

Tough skin

Calloused webs

Huge shoulders

That’s why everyone has left me

I was never enough

My old man

I think he hates me too

He could watch me in pain

He could watch me leave

And he wouldn’t feel a thing

He isn’t proud of what I have become

He’s disappointed in what I haven’t

My friends

They all leave eventually

They grow bored

Of my perpetual and constant ability

To be around

To exist at all times

A symphony I make

For all ears

Nobody care to listen what I play

My fingers toiled at one thing I was good at

Until today

Until every day
