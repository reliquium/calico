why do i

need help with everything

i dance
i sing
and still i don't know how to do
a damn thing

i thought i changed

really
i haven't

i inflate myself
a balloon stretched thin
waiting for the sound
of its own collapse

and when it bursts
nobody minds

i could die tomorrow
and the earth would be lighter

a weight lifted
from its own shoulders

and my head
from mine

i don't talk

i'm afraid of what other people think
when they look at me

i don't do enough

everything i am
exists inside
a false sense of identity

i act intelligent
i pretend i know so much

then someone asks me to prove it
and i reach for something else
anything else

something that can speak
for me

i'm dirty on the inside

the grime

it eats away
at my consciousness

i don't know how to think
when i place a question
in front of myself

a question of what

i don't know

it could be anything

a song
a creation
a work of art

nothing escapes
the tips of my fingers
my tongue
my eyes
my teeth

i sit there
and surrender
before i've even begun

and when i finally try

when i think
i've found something

i stop

i don't know why

my jaw hangs loose

a twelve gauge remington
chambered with buckshot

the thought tears through my skull
through gray matter
through everything i thought
i was

it doesn't stop
until it's finished

it's all so unfair

why couldn't it have been
another time

another place

a different beginning

i could learn
from the ground up

but i'm afraid
of losing comfort

afraid of anything
that requires

a steady back
tough skin
calloused hands
broad shoulders

maybe that's why
everyone leaves

maybe i was never enough

my old man

i think he hates me too

he could watch me hurt
watch me leave

and i don't think
he'd feel a thing

he isn't proud
of what i've become

he's disappointed
in what i haven't

my friends

they leave eventually

they grow tired
of my perpetual existence

my constant presence

the noise i make
just by being here

a symphony
i play for every ear

and nobody
stays long enough
to listen

my fingers
toiled at the one thing
i thought i was good at

until today

until every day
